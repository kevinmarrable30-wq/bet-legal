# BET Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 19, 2026

BET ("the app") is published by Kevin Marrable. Contact: Kevin.Marrable30@protonmail.com

## Data stored on your device
BET stores your profile (such as weight, goals, and training preferences), workout logs, meal logs, macro targets, budget entries, and app settings on your device only. We do not run accounts or servers that receive this data, and we cannot see it.

## Camera
The camera is used only to scan food barcodes. Images are not saved, stored, or sent anywhere.

## Third-party services
When you scan a barcode, the app sends the barcode number to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) to look up nutrition information. As with any internet request, Open Food Facts can see your IP address and basic device and connection details. The app also identifies itself in the request with its name, version, and a contact email for the developer. We do not send your profile, logs, or any other personal data. Open Food Facts has its own privacy policy.

## Notifications
Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. We do not send push notifications from a server.

## Purchases
If you subscribe to BET Pro, the purchase is processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

## Advertising and tracking
BET contains no ads and does not track you across other apps or websites.

## Health disclaimer
BET provides general fitness and nutrition information and is not medical advice. Consult a qualified professional before changing your diet or exercise routine.

## Children
BET is not directed at children under 13.

## Deleting your data
Deleting the app removes the data it stores on your device. Copies may remain in your own device or iCloud backups until those backups are deleted.

## Changes
We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
