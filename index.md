# BET Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 20, 2026

BET ("the app") is published by Kevin Marrable. Contact: FoodScanner.js

## Data stored on your device
BET stores your profile (such as weight, goals, and training preferences), workout logs, meal logs, macro targets, budget entries, and app settings on your device only. We do not run accounts or servers that receive this data, and we cannot see it.

## Camera
The camera is used only to scan food barcodes. Images are not saved, stored, or sent anywhere.

## Third-party services
Barcode lookups. When you scan a food barcode, BET sends the barcode number to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), a free, community-run food database, to look up its nutrition information. The request also includes a User-Agent header that identifies the app and its version and contains the developer's contact email address (FoodScanner.js), so Open Food Facts can reach the developer if there is a problem. This means the developer's contact email address, not yours, is shared with Open Food Facts, a third party. Open Food Facts can also see your IP address and basic device and connection details, as any website or service you connect to can. The camera image itself is never saved or sent anywhere. Open Food Facts' own privacy policy applies to its handling of these requests.

## Notifications
Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. We do not send push notifications from a server.

## Subscriptions and purchases
BET offers an optional subscription, BET Pro. Payments are processed entirely by Apple, and we never see your payment details. Your subscription is managed through your Apple ID settings (Settings > your name > Subscriptions), where you can view, change or cancel it at any time. BET also has a "Manage subscription" button in Settings that opens Apple's subscription settings.

To confirm whether your subscription is active and to restore purchases, BET uses RevenueCat, a subscription management service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID generated for your installation, your purchase and subscription status (including your Apple receipt and purchase history), your device type and operating system, your device's locale and currency code, and the last time you used the app. As with any internet connection, RevenueCat's servers can also see your IP address. RevenueCat does not receive your name, email address, workout data, nutrition data, weight or budget information, which stay on your device. BET does not use accounts or log-ins. You can read RevenueCat's privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

## Advertising and tracking
BET contains no ads and does not track you across other apps or websites.

## Health disclaimer
BET provides general fitness and nutrition information and is not medical advice. Consult a qualified professional before changing your diet or exercise routine.

## Children
BET is not directed at children under 13.

## Deleting your data
Deleting the app removes the data it stores on your device. Copies may remain in your own device or iCloud backups until those backups are deleted. RevenueCat may keep anonymous purchase records for your installation after the app is deleted. To ask about those records, contact the email address below.

## Changes
We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
